Next up

  1. OpenTelemetry export — traces straight into Langfuse or LangSmith
  2. A hosted control plane — the catalog, policy engine and audit trail behind the interfaces the library already defines
  3. Model-written synthesis — richer specs drafted from the request
  4. Streaming responses — progress as the mesh runs, not just at the end

Design principles

Consume, don't rebuild

LangGraph runs the graph, MCP provides connectors, DuckDB reads files, SQLAlchemy talks to databases. AgentMesh decides which agents exist and who may use them.

Never fake success

No silent fallback to a mock. Contract violations are reported. Failed tools are surfaced to the model, not swallowed.

Your budget, your call

No default spending cap of any kind.
